# Corpus Compendium on Rails

*A Unified Parallel-Theory and Coupled-Mechanics Redesign of 80 Quantitative Fields*

By Russell Philip Smithson. Copyright (C) 2026. All rights reserved.

The compendium states 4,805 equations from 80 quantitative fields. Each entry runs on four rails:

1. **Classical statement**, the equation as its own field writes it, with symbols, origin and use.
2. **Linear-algebraic form**, the same fact in vectors, matrices and operators, with a status line saying whether the translation is exact, a linearisation, a structural analogue, or impossible.
3. **8S coupled-mechanics image**, the image under the 8S Penteract–S³ master coupled-mechanics law, with a six-step derivation (P1)–(P6).
4. **Continuum image**, the image under the calculus transform, with a six-step derivation (Q1)–(Q6).

## Corrected edition and scope

This edition was corrected on 15 November 2026 after an accuracy and validity audit. Sixteen factual errors in the field entries were fixed, and Part 0 now claims only what it proves:

- The retraction of Theorem J returns an input law exactly when its operator is at most 3 × 3, and identity (0) holds up to *O*(ℓ²), plus *O*(σ²) for ℒ⋆.
- Rails III and IV are a construction applied class by class rather than an independent proof for each entry. Their retraction step returns the input equation for class T4, and for small operators in class T1.
- The admission rule's ingredients (Score, ℳ₇, ℳ₈, Classify and the thresholds) are not specified, so the admission steps cannot be evaluated.

Rails I and II carry the content a practitioner can check. Rails III and IV should be read as a systematic re-expression of each entry in one framework, within the limits stated above. See [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md) for the full list, including the issues not yet fixed.
